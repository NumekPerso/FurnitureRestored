# Furniture Restored

An unofficial port of **MrCrayfish's Furniture Mod** to Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge), bringing back the furniture of the 1.12.2 version.

## Why this project

The Furniture Mod was rebuilt from scratch for the newer versions of Minecraft. Many pieces of furniture from the 1.12.2 version never made it to the new version: the kitchen appliances, the bathroom, the electronics, the modern furniture, the Christmas decorations and more.

Furniture Restored starts from the official 1.20.1 version, 7.0.0-pre36, and adds that content back, so that players can use it in the current version of the game.

## What it adds

- **Kitchen:** oven, range hood, toaster, microwave, blender, washing machine, dishwasher, plates, cups, cookie jar, chopping board, bar stools, kitchen cabinets.
- **Bathroom:** toilet, basin, bath, shower, wall cabinet, bin, mirror.
- **Electronics:** computer, TVs, TV stand, printer, stereo, lamps and lights, ceiling fan, light switch, door bell, digital clock, fire alarm, electric fence.
- **Decoration:** curtains, modern couch, bed, table, chair and stairs, sliding door, window, glass table, bird bath, inflatable castle, photo frame.
- **Christmas:** presents, tree, mantel piece, grand chair, candles, chimney, wreath, fairy lights, fire pit.
- **Stone furniture and door mats** in every colour.
- In the creative tab: a search bar and a banner above each category.
- When you hold a piece of furniture, a white outline shows where it will be placed.

Some 1.12.2 features are not included: the MineBay shop, working TVs, working photo frames, and mirror reflections. These blocks are decoration only.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.0.14 or later

The mod ID is `furniture_restored`. Worlds made with the official Furniture Mod do not load their furniture in Furniture Restored.

## Building

```
./gradlew build
```

The jar, `FurnitureRestored-forge-1.20.1-1.0.jar`, is written to `build/libs`.
